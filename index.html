Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Surendra.in ("we", "our" or "us") provides the UPI Soundbox application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the app.

1. Information We Process

  • Account information: Information associated with your Google or email login through Firebase Authentication.
  • Cloud data: Information stored in Firebase Firestore, as applicable to the features you use.
  • SMS messages: If SMS permission is enabled, the app may process payment-related SMS messages to identify payment alerts and announce them.
  • Notifications: If notification access is enabled, the app may process relevant notifications from supported payment apps to provide voice alerts.
  • Local history: Payment alert history may be stored on your device, depending on the app implementation.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide login and account-related features.
  • Detect relevant payment alerts from permitted sources.
  • Announce detected alerts using text-to-speech.
  • Store and display app data where supported.
  • Maintain security and improve app functionality.

3. SMS and Notification Permissions

SMS and notification access are used only for the features described above. Please grant these permissions only if you understand and agree to their use. You can manage permissions through your device settings.

4. Payment Verification Disclaimer

UPI Soundbox announces payment-related messages or notifications detected on your device. Unless explicitly provided by a verified integration, the app does not independently verify transactions with a bank, UPI network, or payment provider. Always check your official bank or payment app before relying on a payment alert.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage where applicable. These services may process information under their own privacy terms.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can request account deletion using the Delete Account & Data link on this page. The deletion process should remove your Firebase Authentication account and associated app data according to the app's implemented deletion procedure. Data stored locally on your device may require separate removal if it is not deleted by the app.

The app is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us.
