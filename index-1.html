1. Introduction

UPI Soundbox: Voice Alerts (“App”) is an Android application that announces payment-related notifications and SMS messages through voice alerts. This policy explains how information may be accessed, processed, stored, and deleted when you use the App.

The App does not independently verify payments with banks, UPI networks, or payment providers. A voice announcement is not proof that money has been received.

2. Information We Process

  • Account information: email address and Firebase Authentication user ID used for Google Sign-In or email login.
  • Payment-related information: relevant text from supported UPI notifications and bank SMS messages. Messages may contain amounts, sender details, transaction references, and timestamps.
  • App settings: voice, language, and other preferences.
  • Local history: payment-related records saved locally on your device by the App.

The information available depends on the content of the notification or message.

3. Notification Access

If you enable Android Notification Access, the App may access notifications from supported payment applications and process relevant content to generate voice announcements. Notifications can contain personal or financial information. You can revoke this permission in Android settings; notification-based alerts may then stop working.

4. SMS Access

The App uses SMS access to detect relevant payment-related SMS messages and generate voice announcements. Relevant details may include payment amounts, sender information, transaction references, and timestamps when present. The intended use is payment-alert detection, not general SMS management. Android may technically grant broader access than the specific messages needed, so the App should process only messages relevant to its stated function. You can revoke SMS permission in Android settings; SMS-based alerts may then stop working. SMS access is subject to applicable laws, Android requirements, and Google Play policies.

5. How We Use Information

  • To announce supported payment notifications and SMS messages.
  • To identify available payment-related details in messages.
  • To provide Google and email authentication.
  • To maintain app preferences and local payment history.
  • To provide applicable cloud functionality through Firebase.

The App does not independently check your bank account or confirm that a transaction has settled.

6. Data Storage

Firebase Authentication: supports Google Sign-In and email login.

Firebase Firestore: stores applicable account-related or app data required for cloud functionality.

Local device storage: payment history is intended to be stored locally on your device.

Payment notification and SMS content is processed for voice alerts. This policy does not claim that this content is uploaded to Firebase unless the App's implemented features actually transmit it. Local history should not be assumed to be backed up to the cloud.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Firebase is used for authentication and applicable cloud functionality. Information needed for those services may be processed by Google/Firebase under their applicable terms and privacy policies. This policy should be updated if advertising, analytics, or other third-party integrations are added.

8. Retention

Account information and applicable cloud data may be retained while your account is active and as needed to operate the App. Local history may remain on your device until deleted through the App, cleared through device settings, or removed by the account-deletion process, depending on the implementation. Information required to be retained by law may be handled separately as legally required.

9. Account and Data Deletion

The App provides a Delete Account option. The deletion process is intended to remove your Firebase Authentication account, associated cloud data maintained by the App in Firestore, and payment history and app data stored locally on your device. The App should confirm completion only after the required deletion operations succeed. If deletion fails, follow the in-app instructions or contact support. Legally required retention, if any, may apply.

Open the Account & Data Deletion page.

10. Security

We aim to protect information using appropriate safeguards and the security features available through Android and Firebase. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and account, and avoid sharing screenshots containing sensitive payment information.

11. Your Choices

You can revoke Notification Access and SMS permission, change supported app preferences, sign out, and request account deletion. Revoking permissions may disable related features.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact the developer.

13. International Users

The App may be available worldwide. Information processed by Firebase may be handled in locations where its services operate, subject to applicable terms and legal requirements. Users are responsible for complying with laws that apply to their use of the App.

14. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the App's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version should be published with a revised effective date.
